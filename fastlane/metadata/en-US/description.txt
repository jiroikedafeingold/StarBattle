Cherry Battle is a clean, friendly take on Star Battle — the elegant "two-not-touch" logic puzzle.

The rules are simple, the deductions are deep: place two cherries in every row, every column and every coloured region. The catch — no two cherries may ever touch, not even diagonally. From those few rules grows a surprisingly rich puzzle you solve with pure logic.

Every board is generated fresh on your device with exactly one solution, and the game favours puzzles you can crack by reasoning alone — so you never have to guess.

WHY YOU'LL LIKE IT
• Five difficulties — a gentle Beginner board to learn on, then Easy, Medium, Hard, and a brutal new Expert that leans on the hardest technique again and again.
• Hints that teach — stuck on a square? A hint places the move logic forces, names the technique behind it, and explains when and why that technique works — so you learn new tactics instead of just getting the answer.
• Mark mode — try a "what if" on a scratch layer: pencil in a line of candidate guesses, follow the logic, then commit them in one satisfying sweep.
• Smart helpers — placing a cherry automatically dots its neighbours, drag to sweep dots down a line, and Undo or Redo any move.
• Share a board — send a friend a link that opens the exact same puzzle to race or compare.
• Track your progress — games solved, solve rate, best & average times, hints used and mistakes caught, per difficulty.
• Make it yours — a light or dark theme, a quiet timer you can hide, and a playing piece you pick: cherry, star, robot, unicorn, dino and more.
• Make it playful — optional sound effects, a fun hop-and-wiggle piece animation, and a live mistake-checker that flags rule-breaking pieces the moment you place them.
• iPhone, iPad & Mac — an adaptive board that fills the screen on any device, in portrait or landscape.
• Sweet victory — solve it and the board bursts into a shower of pieces and a celebratory rumble of haptics.
• No ads, no accounts, no tracking — and it works completely offline.
